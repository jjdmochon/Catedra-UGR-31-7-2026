# Concept: Abasic Probes

Abasic probes are Peptide Nucleic Acid (PNA) sequences that contain a 'blank' or abasic position (typically replacing a base with an abasic monomer). When the probe hybridizes to a target DNA/RNA, a vacant pocket is created opposite the target base. This pocket selectively binds a smart nucleobase or label via dynamic covalent chemistry, achieving single-base genotyping resolution.
