# Concept: Click Chemistry

Click Chemistry describes a class of biocompatible reactions that are fast, high-yielding, and highly selective under mild conditions. The copper-catalyzed azide-alkyne cycloaddition (CuAAC) and copper-free strain-promoted cycloaddition are widely used in Diaz-Mochon's research to conjugate functionalized nanoparticles, PNA probes, and bioorthogonal tags inside cells.
