# Concept: Dynamic Chemical Labeling (DCL)

Dynamic Chemical Labeling (DCL) utilizes reversible dynamic covalent chemistry (such as aldehyde-amine condensation) to label and identify specific target sequences without enzymatic amplification. In nucleic acid sensing, abasic PNA probes incorporate a chemical tag (like a fluorescent dye or biotin) via an enzyme-free dynamic ligation reaction at the target site.
