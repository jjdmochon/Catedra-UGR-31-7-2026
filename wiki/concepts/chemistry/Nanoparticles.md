# Concept: Nanoparticles

Functionalized nanoparticles and nanosystems (such as polymer nanoparticles, metallic nanoparticles, or tracker nanocatalysts) are widely utilized in Diaz-Mochon's research as carriers for targeted drug delivery, imaging agents, and platforms for bioorthogonal click chemistry reactions.
