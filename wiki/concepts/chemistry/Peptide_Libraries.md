# Concept: Peptide Libraries

High-throughput combinatorial peptide and peptoid libraries (often encoded with Peptide Nucleic Acids) are synthesized on solid supports or microarrays to profile enzyme substrates, identify cellular binding ligands, and screen protease/kinase activities.
