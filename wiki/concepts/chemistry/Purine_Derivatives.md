# Concept: Purine Derivatives

Substituted purine and adenine derivatives are synthesized and evaluated as biologically active compounds, acting as adenosine receptor ligands, pro-apoptotic inducers of leukemic lymphocytes, and inhibitors of kinases like DAPK-1.
