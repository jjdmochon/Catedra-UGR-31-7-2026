# Concept: Solid-Phase Synthesis

Solid-Phase Peptide Synthesis (SPPS) and combinatorial chemistry involve building polymer, peptide, or PNA chains step-by-step on solid supports (like resin beads). This method allows the generation of diverse combinatorial libraries (such as PNA-encoded peptide libraries) for high-throughput screening of cellular binders and enzyme substrates.
