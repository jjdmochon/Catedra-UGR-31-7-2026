# Concept: Cell-Free DNA (cfDNA)

Cell-free DNA (cfDNA) analysis in blood plasma is a cornerstone of non-invasive liquid biopsy, utilized for cancer profiling, treatment response monitoring, and early detection of disease recurrence in clinical practice.
