# Concept: Circulating Tumor Cells (CTCs)

Circulating Tumor Cells (CTCs) are cells that shed from a primary tumor into the vasculature, serving as critical biomarkers in liquid biopsies for cancer monitoring. Techniques like miRNA in-situ hybridization are developed to detect and profile CTCs.
