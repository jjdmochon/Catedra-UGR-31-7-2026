# Concept: Hepatotoxicity (DILI)

Hepatotoxicity and Drug-Induced Liver Injury (DILI) are major safety issues in clinical drug development and medical toxicology. Research focuses on the direct detection of microRNA-122 (miR-122), a highly sensitive and specific circulating liver biomarker, to track liver injury and recovery (e.g., from paracetamol overdose) without PCR artifacts.
