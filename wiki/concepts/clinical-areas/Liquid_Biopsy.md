# Concept: Liquid Biopsy

Liquid Biopsy is a minimally invasive diagnostic technique that analyzes biomarkers in blood or biofluids, such as circulating tumor cells (CTCs), cell-free DNA (cfDNA), and microRNAs (like miR-21). This research develops platforms to capture, isolate, and molecularly profile CTCs and nucleic acids to monitor cancer therapy, recurrence, and early interception.
