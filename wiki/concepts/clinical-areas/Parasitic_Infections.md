# Concept: Parasitic Infections

This clinical area targets the detection and species-specific differentiation of tropical parasites, specifically Leishmania (causing mucocutaneous and cutaneous leishmaniasis) and Trypanosoma cruzi (causing Chagas disease). Low-cost, colorimetric assays are developed for rapid species identification.
