# Concept: Tuberculosis

Tuberculosis (TB) and related mycobacterioses are infectious diseases caused by Mycobacterium tuberculosis. Research includes developing DestiNA 'spin-tube' assays and other rapid molecular tests for the detection and differentiation of TB.
