# Concept: Viral Infections

This clinical focus covers rapid diagnostic tools for viral detection (such as SARS-CoV-2/COVID-19 and influenza) using mobile apps and dynamic chemistry. It also includes tracking viral replication and profiling inflammation biomarkers directly from circulating blood cells.
