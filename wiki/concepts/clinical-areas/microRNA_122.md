# Concept: microRNA-122 (miR-122)

MicroRNA-122 is a highly abundant, liver-specific microRNA that serves as a sensitive circulating biomarker for drug-induced liver injury (DILI) and hepatotoxicity. Direct chemical labeling enables its measurement in clinical settings without PCR amplification.
