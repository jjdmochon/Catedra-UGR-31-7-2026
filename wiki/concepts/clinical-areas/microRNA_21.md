# Concept: microRNA-21 (miR-21)

MicroRNA-21 is a well-known oncogenic microRNA frequently overexpressed in solid tumors. It is targeted as a diagnostic biomarker in liquid biopsies, detected directly from tumor cells or biofluids using chemistry-based, PCR-free platforms.
