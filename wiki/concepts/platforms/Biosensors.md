# Concept: Biosensors

Biosensors and biochips integrate biochemical recognition elements (like abasic PNA probes) with physical transducers (such as colorimetric sensors, fluorescent imaging, or smartphone readers) to directly detect circulating biomarkers without PCR amplification.
