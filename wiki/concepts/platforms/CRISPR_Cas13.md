# Concept: CRISPR-Cas13

CRISPR-Cas13 is an RNA-guided gene-editing and diagnostic effector that exhibits collateral RNA cleavage activity upon target binding. Diaz-Mochon's work develops dual-guide CRISPR-Cas13 systems to improve mismatch discrimination, providing highly specific molecular diagnostics for single-nucleotide variants.
