# Concept: Flow Cytometry

Flow Cytometry is a laser-based biophysical technology used for cell counting, cell sorting, and biomarker detection. Diaz-Mochon's research utilizes flow cytometry to analyze cell uptake of functionalized nanoparticles and perform capture/detection of nucleic acids using bead-based probes.
