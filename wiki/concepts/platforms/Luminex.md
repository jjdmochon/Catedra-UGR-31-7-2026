# Concept: Luminex

The Luminex platform (including MAGPIX and FLEXMAP 3D) is a multiplexing bead-based assay system that uses color-coded magnetic microspheres. Diaz-Mochon's research integrates dynamic chemistry on Luminex beads, enabling direct, enzyme-free multiplexed detection of microRNAs and proteins directly from biofluids.
