# Concept: Polymer Microarrays

Polymer microarrays are high-throughput cell culture platforms where hundreds of distinct polymer formulations are printed on a single glass slide. These biochips are used to screen cell-material interactions, study stem cell differentiation, and identify biocompatible substrates for transfection or vascular devices.
