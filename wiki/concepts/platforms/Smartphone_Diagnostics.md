# Concept: Smartphone Diagnostics

Smartphone-based diagnostic systems utilize mobile phone cameras to capture and interpret colorimetric color changes from molecular assays (e.g. CoVradar tubes and the CoVreader app). This platform provides low-cost, portable, point-of-care diagnostics for viral and parasitic infections in resource-limited settings.
