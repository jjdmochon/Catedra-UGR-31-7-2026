# Institution: Servicio Andaluz de Salud

The Servicio Andaluz de Salud (SAS) is the public health provider in Andalusia, Spain. It partners with academic institutions on translational medicine, clinical validation of oncology biomarkers, and point-of-care viral detection assays.
