# Institution: University of Edinburgh

The University of Edinburgh is a world-renowned research university in Scotland. Diaz-Mochon's early research on solid supports, combinatorial peptide libraries, and polymer microarrays was developed there in collaboration with Mark Bradley and Asier Unciti-Broceta.
