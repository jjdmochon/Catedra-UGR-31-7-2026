# Project/Grant: LIQBIOPSENS

LIQBIOPSENS was a major European Horizon 2020 project (H2020-IA 687785) aimed at developing a novel, reliable, and affordable diagnostic platform for colorectal cancer screening using liquid biopsy (specifically detecting miR-21 and CTCs) without PCR amplification.
