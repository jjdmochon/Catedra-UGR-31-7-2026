# Open Questions and Opportunities

## Overview
This section outlines active scientific questions, literature debates, and strategic opportunities for funding, spin-offs, and pedagogical development within the JJD lab.

---

## 1. Unresolved Scientific Questions & Gaps

### RNA Structural Dynamics & Druggability
- **Conformational States of eRNAs**: How do low-abundance, short-lived enhancer RNAs (such as DHS44500) transition between secondary hairpins and tertiary folds in vivo? Can a small molecule target a single conformation without triggering off-target degradation of structurally similar human host RNAs (tRNAs, rRNAs)?
- **Catalytic Cleavage Efficiency**: What is the minimum local concentration of RiboTAC required to trigger in-situ dimerization and activation of endogenous RNase L? Does the kinetics of cleavage outpace the transcription rate of active super-enhancers?

### Clinical Biomarkers & Analytical Noise
- **miR-122 Isomirs**: Circulating microRNAs frequently exhibit terminal sequence variations (isomirs). Do minor sequence differences in miR-122 reflect different stages of liver recovery in drug-induced liver injury (DILI), or are they analytical noise?
- **Genomic Repeats & SNVs**: How do single nucleotide variations (SNVs) in highly repetitive centromeric alpha-satellite DNA influence chromosome segregation and aging? Can `[[Micro_C_snm3C_seq]]` confirm in situ structural differences resolved by chemFISH?

## 2. Research & Funding Opportunities


### Commercial & Licensing Partnerships
- **DESTINA Chemistry Scaling**: Expand current commercial supply agreements (such as with MilliporeSigma, Optoi, and Mecwins) to offer standardized DCL-based microRNA kits for clinical toxicology and cardiovascular panels.
- **CRISPNA Platforms**: Scale CRISPR-Cas13 diagnostic systems (paired with PNA chemistry) for point-of-care detection of tropical parasites (Leishmania) and emerging viral threats.

## 3. Teaching & Pedagogical Development

### Integrating AI/ML in Grado en Farmacia
- **AI-Guided Lead Discovery**: Implement student workshops in `[[QFUNO]]` utilizing open-source deep learning docking tools (e.g., DiffDock, Boltz2) to predict the binding affinity of student-designed molecules against clinical protein/RNA targets.
- **Translational Medicine Case Studies**: Incorporate raw clinical trial data from the SAS or NHS (e.g., paracetamol hepatotoxicity cohorts) into `[[TRANSMED]]` practicals, training students to compute ROC AUC and set diagnostic cutoff thresholds.
