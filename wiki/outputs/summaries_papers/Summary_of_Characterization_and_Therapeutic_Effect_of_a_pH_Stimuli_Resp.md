Abstract: Despite the large number of polymeric nanodelivery systems that have been recently developed, there is still room for improvement in terms of therapeutic eﬃciency. Most reported nanodevices for controlled release are based on drug encapsulation, which can lead to undesired drug leakage with a consequent reduction in eﬃcacy and an increase in systemic toxicity.
