Polymer hydrogel microarrays were fabricated by inkjet printing of monomers and initiator, allowing up to 1800 individual polymer features to be printed on a single glass slide.

[Hydrogels](../../concepts/chemistry/Hydrogels.md), due to their intrinsic hydration properties have huge potential, with applications in the area of tissue engineering,1–3 and cellular attachment/release.4,5 These ﬂexible gels have also been applied as thermally responsive micropumps/ valves,6 as components in sensors,7,8 as surface actuators9 and for drug release.10
