# Summary of: Open a new window in the world of circulating microRNAs by merging ChemiRNA Tech with a Luminex platform

## 📄 Abstract / Context Preview
[microRNAs](../../concepts/clinical-areas/microRNA_122.md) (miRs) are small noncoding RNA molecules showing huge promise as biomarkers and diagnostic tools for illnesses, as significant changes in their expression occur in response to pathological states. However, multiplex detection of microRNAs represents a major challenge towards the use of microRNA signatures. Hence, our group has tackled this need by developing an accurate and PCR-free approach to detect multiple microRNAs in serum samples. [ChemiRNA Tech](../../concepts/chemistry/Dynamic_Chemical_Labeling.md) has been refined to be merged with a [Luminex](../../concepts/platforms/Luminex.md) xMAP system. The combination of these two technologies has created a unique bead-based multiplex assay capable of simultaneously measuring [miR-122-5p](../../concepts/clinical-areas/microRNA_122.md), miR-451a-5p and miR-193a-5p in only 10 μL of serum. This novel multiplex assay for the direct quantitative measurement of circulating microRNA signatures offers benefits over gold standard singleplex assay techniques in terms of cost, time savings and ease of use.

### Key Concepts & Platforms Featured:
- **[Dynamic Chemical Labeling](../../concepts/chemistry/Dynamic_Chemical_Labeling.md)**: Also referred to as *ChemiRNA Tech*, this is a single-base specific method that enables direct detection and quantification of microRNAs without extraction or pre-amplification.
- **[Abasic Probes](../../concepts/chemistry/Abasic_Probes.md)**: Specifically, DGL-Probes based on modified [Peptide Nucleic Acids](../../concepts/chemistry/Peptide_Nucleic_Acids.md) (PNAs) with abasic positions are utilized to hybridize and capture specific microRNAs.
- **[Luminex](../../concepts/platforms/Luminex.md)**: Uses color-coded MagPlex microspheres to perform multiplexed detection.
- **[Hepatotoxicity](../../concepts/clinical-areas/Hepatotoxicity.md)**: Used in the diagnostic profiling of [microRNA-122](../../concepts/clinical-areas/microRNA_122.md) (miR-122-5p), which is a key biomarker for Drug-Induced Liver Injury (DILI).
- **Other Targets**: miR-451a-5p (erythroid cell-specific biomarker, linked to hemolysis) and miR-193a-5p (implicated in liver injury and carcinogenesis).
