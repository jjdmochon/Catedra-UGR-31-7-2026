## 📄 Patent Abstract / Overview
This patent describes a novel dual-guide RNA composition that improves the specificity of [CRISPR-Cas13](../../concepts/platforms/CRISPR_Cas13.md) systems. By utilizing two distinct RNA guides (dcrRNA and dtracrRNA), the system significantly enhances target mismatch discrimination, enabling precise single-nucleotide variant (SNV) detection for molecular diagnostics.
