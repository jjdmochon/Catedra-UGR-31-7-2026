## 📄 Patent Abstract / Overview
This patent describes a multiplexed biodetection system that combines optical measurement platforms with [dynamic chemical labelling](../../concepts/chemistry/Dynamic_Chemical_Labeling.md) for the highly sensitive detection of target biomarkers, such as microRNAs and proteins. It provides improved diagnostics by reducing background noise and multiplexing measurements on a single sample.
