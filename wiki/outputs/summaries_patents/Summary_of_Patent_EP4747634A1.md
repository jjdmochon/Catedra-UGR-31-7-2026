## 📄 Patent Abstract / Overview
This patent describes surface-functionalized nanoparticles optimized for targeted drug delivery and high-contrast bioimaging. The functionalized nanoparticles are designed to target cancer cells specifically, delivering diagnostic markers or therapeutic agents ([theranostics](../../concepts/platforms/Theranostics.md)) directly to tumor cells with reduced systemic toxicity.
