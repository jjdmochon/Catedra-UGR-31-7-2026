## 📄 Patent Abstract / Overview
This patent describes multifunctional nanoparticles designed for teragnosis ([theranostics](../../concepts/platforms/Theranostics.md)). The polymer-based nanoparticles are engineered to encapsulate therapeutic oncology drugs and carry fluorescent tags, enabling simultaneous targeted cancer therapy and real-time visualization of drug distribution.
