## 📄 Patent Abstract / Overview
This patent details a diagnostic method to detect and profile viruses and bacteria (such as [SARS-CoV-2](../../concepts/clinical-areas/Viral_Infections.md)) inside circulating epithelial cells isolated from peripheral blood. It acts as a biomarker platform for tracking severe inflammatory or infectious disease states and monitoring patient recovery.
