## 📄 Patent Abstract / Overview
This patent describes an improved abasic [Peptide Nucleic Acid](../../concepts/chemistry/Peptide_Nucleic_Acids.md) (PNA) probe with optimized backbone structures and sequence lengths. The probe is engineered to bind specifically to target microRNAs or SNPs, providing superior single-nucleotide mismatch discrimination in chemical-labeling assays.
