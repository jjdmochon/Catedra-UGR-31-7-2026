## 📄 Patent Abstract / Overview
This patent describes the foundational technology for nucleobase characterisation using abasic [peptide nucleic acids](../../concepts/chemistry/Peptide_Nucleic_Acids.md) (PNAs) and smart nucleobase labelling. The method enables target identification and sequencing with single-nucleotide resolution without the need for enzymatic amplification.
