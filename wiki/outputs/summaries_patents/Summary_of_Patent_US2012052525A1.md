## 📄 Patent Abstract / Overview
This patent describes [polymer microarrays](../../concepts/platforms/Polymer_Microarrays.md) and compositions designed to identify optimal substrates for mammalian cell growth and transfection. It uses combinatorial polymer library screening to identify biocompatible materials that enhance cell viability and transfection efficiency.
