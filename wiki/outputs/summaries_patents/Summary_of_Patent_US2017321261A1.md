## 📄 Patent Abstract / Overview
This patent details a diagnostic method for detecting [circulating tumor cells](../../concepts/clinical-areas/Circulating_Tumor_Cells.md) (CTCs) exhibiting both epithelial and mesenchymal-to-epithelial transition (EMT) phenotypes. The method utilizes [microRNA-21](../../concepts/clinical-areas/microRNA_21.md) as a highly specific biomarker to track cancer progression and metastasis.
