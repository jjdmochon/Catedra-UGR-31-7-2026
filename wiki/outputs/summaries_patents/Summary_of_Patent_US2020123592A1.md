## 📄 Patent Abstract / Overview
This patent describes a method for the single-molecule detection and quantification of microRNAs with single-base specificity. Using abasic [Peptide Nucleic Acids](../../concepts/chemistry/Peptide_Nucleic_Acids.md) (PNA) and [dynamic chemical labelling](../../concepts/chemistry/Dynamic_Chemical_Labeling.md) on single-molecule arrays ([Simoa](../../concepts/platforms/Single_Molecule_Arrays.md)), it enables sensitive diagnosis of [drug-induced liver injury](../../concepts/clinical-areas/Hepatotoxicity.md) and other clinical conditions.
