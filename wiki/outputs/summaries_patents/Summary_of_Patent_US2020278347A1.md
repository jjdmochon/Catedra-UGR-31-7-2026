## 📄 Patent Abstract / Overview
This patent describes methods and devices for isolating circulating cells of epithelial origin ([circulating tumor cells](../../concepts/clinical-areas/Circulating_Tumor_Cells.md) or CTCs) from peripheral blood. Using specialized polymer microfilters and microfluidic channels, the technology allows for clean isolation of rare cells for downstream analysis and [liquid biopsy](../../concepts/clinical-areas/Liquid_Biopsy.md).
