## 📄 Patent Abstract / Overview
This patent describes the 'CRISPNA' platform, which combines CRISPR-Cas genome editing tools with [Peptide Nucleic Acids](../../concepts/chemistry/Peptide_Nucleic_Acids.md) (PNA). This hybrid platform achieves superior target specificity and editing efficiency, minimizing off-target genomic cleavage and improving diagnostics.
