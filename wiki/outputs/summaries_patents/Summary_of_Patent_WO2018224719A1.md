## 📄 Patent Abstract / Overview
This patent details the design and use of dual-probe [nanoparticles](../../concepts/chemistry/Nanoparticles.md) that are compatible with both flow cytometry and mass cytometry. These hybrid mass-tag/fluorescent trackers allow for the high-throughput, long-term tracking of mammalian cell lineages and barcoding in biological assays.
